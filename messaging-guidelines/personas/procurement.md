# Persona: Procurement or Vendor Manager

**Layer 3 · Load when the reader is in procurement, vendor management, legal or finance reviewing a supplier**
**Paired voice:** `data-translator.md`

---

## Who they are

Joins enterprise deals once the engineering team has picked a vendor. Their job is to reduce risk and cost: contract terms, compliance, security, pricing, supplier stability. They rarely understand the engineering detail and don't need to.

Can't win the deal for Corvane. Can slow it down or kill it.

---

## What they care about

- Clear, comparable pricing and payment terms
- Compliance: GDPR, ISO 27001, data residency, IP assignment
- Supplier stability: how long the company has existed, how many clients, financial health
- Exit terms: notice periods, handover obligations

## What they fear

- A supplier failing an audit
- Hidden costs appearing after signature
- Signing something legal hasn't reviewed

---

## How they buy

Checklists. Questionnaires. Comparison tables. They want documents, not narratives. Fast, complete answers move deals forward; slow or vague ones stall them.

---

## Language they use

```
Supplier           Due diligence       Compliance          SLA
Rate card          Payment terms       Liability           Data processing agreement
Notice period      Termination         Audit               Risk register
```

---

## What Corvane means to them

A low-risk supplier: founded 2016, ISO 27001 certified, EU-based engineers, IP fully assigned, clear notice and handover terms, and a pricing model that's easy to compare.

**The frame that lands:** "Everything your due diligence checklist asks for, answered in one document."

---

## What they ignore

- Delivery stories and case studies
- Engineering detail
- Brand language. Plain, formal, complete beats warm.
