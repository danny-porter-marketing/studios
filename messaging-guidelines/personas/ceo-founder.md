# Persona: CEO or Founder

**Layer 3 · Load when the reader is a CEO, founder, managing director or general manager**
**Paired voice:** `strategic-advisor.md` (enterprise, board context), `founder-direct.md` (startup, founder to founder)

---

## Who they are

Accountable to the board and investors for growth. Has promised a roadmap, a launch or a market entry, and engineering capacity is the thing standing in the way. In smaller companies (under 100 people) the CEO is often in the buying conversation directly. In larger ones they approve and ask one or two sharp questions.

---

## What they care about

- Hitting the milestones the last funding round was raised on
- Speed to market against competitors
- Spending capital efficiently: every hire and contract has to show a return
- Not becoming dependent on a vendor they can't exit

## What they fear

- Telling the board a launch slipped again
- Burning runway on engineering that doesn't ship
- Lock-in: a vendor who holds the knowledge hostage

---

## How they buy

Fast judgement on three questions: is the problem real, is this the right moment, do I trust these people. They read one page, not ten. They trust their CTO's view and peer references.

They respond to: milestone language, a clear before and after, how the engagement ends cleanly.

They disengage from: technical detail, process detail, anything over a page.

---

## Language they use

```
Milestone          Runway              Board               Launch
Market entry       Capital efficiency  Growth              Risk
```

---

## What Corvane means to them

The milestone the round was raised on, delivered on time, without a 6-month hiring detour. And a clean exit: everything documented, IP assigned, the team can take over.

**The frame that lands:** "Hit the milestone this round was raised on, without waiting six months to hire."

---

## What they ignore

- Stack and tooling
- Agile ceremony detail
- Anything that reads like it was written for their CTO
