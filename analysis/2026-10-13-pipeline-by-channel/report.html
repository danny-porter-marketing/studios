What the data says

  1. Partner referrals are 6% of leads (84) but 33 of 115 SQLs, 45% of pipeline value (€2.84M) and 18 of 36 wins, at €242 per SQL.
  2. LI_AI_Delivery_Q2 spent €41k for 151 leads and 109 MQLs, and produced zero SQLs. It is the clearest case of leads without pipeline.
  3. LinkedIn Ads brings the most leads (420) and 39% of all MQLs, but has the highest cost per SQL of any channel at €4,333.
  4. Case studies as a first asset convert to SQL at 15.3%, against 6.0% for everything else. Blogs and the AI webinar convert at 3.0%.
  5. CTOs and VPs of Engineering convert to SQL at about 13% and make up 52% of SQLs. Developers (2.7%) and marketing managers (0.9%) barely convert.

Notes

Recommended next steps

  • Pause LI_AI_Delivery_Q2 and review GA_Outsourcing_Generic (€6,800 per SQL). Move the budget into partner programme and CTO-targeted case study campaigns.
  • Make case studies the default offer in paid and retargeting, and stop targeting developer and marketing titles.
  • Report weekly on SQLs, pipeline value and cost per SQL instead of leads and MQLs.

What this data can't tell us

  • Win counts are small outside partners (3 to 5 per channel), so revenue by channel is directional.
  • first_asset and source are first touch only. Nothing here shows what moved a deal later.
  • Data ends 12 Sep. August and September leads (230) have had little time to reach SQL.
  • Spend is one Jan to Sep total per campaign, so efficiency can't be tracked by month.
  • 21 leads at SQL stage have no deal value yet, so pipeline value slightly understates the total.

What was cleaned and why

  • Removed 12 exact duplicate rows (1,412 to 1,400). No lead_id conflicts remained.
  • Mapped 18 source spellings to 6 channels (954 rows renamed).
  • Converted 204 DD/MM/YYYY dates to ISO. 121 have a day above 12, which confirms the format.
  • Converted 5 deal values stored as text (e.g. '€56,500') to numbers.
  • Recased 43 job titles. Set 58 blank regions to 'Unknown' and 677 blank campaigns to '(none)'.
  • Added 1/0 flags for MQL, SQL, Opportunity and won. Later stages count as reaching earlier ones.
  • Pipeline value is the deal value of Opportunity, Closed Won and Closed Lost leads (the only stages with a value).
