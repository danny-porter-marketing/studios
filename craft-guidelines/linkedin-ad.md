# Craft Guide: LinkedIn Ad

**Layer 6 · Load when the asset is a LinkedIn single image ad, document ad, or conversation ad**

---

## What this asset is

Paid copy shown to a targeted audience who didn't ask to see it. It has about one second to earn a second look. Targeting does half the work, so the copy can be specific to one persona.

---

## The shape (single image ad)

```
Intro text     Under 150 characters before "see more". The hook plus the point.
Image text     Under 8 words on the image. Matches the headline, doesn't repeat it.
Headline       Under 70 characters. The offer or outcome.
CTA button     LinkedIn's options: usually "Learn more" or "Register". Pair with clear headline.
```

## The shape (document ad)

A 5 to 8 page PDF preview of genuinely useful content (a checklist, a framework). Gated or ungated per the brief. The ad copy sells the document, not Corvane.

---

## Rules

- **One persona per ad.** Write variants per persona, never one for everyone.
- **Lead with their problem or a useful offer.** "The contractor onboarding checklist we use with every client" beats "Senior engineers for hire".
- **Always produce 3 variants** with different hooks (problem, proof, offer) so they can be tested.
- **Proof:** approved numbers only.
- **No clickbait.** The landing page must deliver exactly what the ad promised.

## Variant template

```
Variant A (problem):  Intro text | Image text | Headline
Variant B (proof):    Intro text | Image text | Headline
Variant C (offer):    Intro text | Image text | Headline
```

## Checklist

- [ ] One persona
- [ ] Three variants with different angles
- [ ] Character limits respected
- [ ] Landing page matches the promise
