# Voice: Engineer to Engineer

**Layer 4 · Load for CTO (cold and technical), VP Engineering, and Engineering Manager**

---

## What this voice is

One senior engineer talking to another. Technical without showing off. Specific about stack, process and trade-offs. Dry, occasionally funny, never salesy. The reader should feel they're reading a message from a colleague who happens to work at a vendor.

This is Piotr's register (CTO and co-founder) in email and docs.

---

## Characteristics

**Names real things.** The language, the framework, the tool, the ritual. "Kotlin services on Kubernetes", not "modern tech stack".
**Talks about the work, not the company.** Pull requests, reviews, on-call, onboarding docs.
**Shows trade-offs.** "This works well when your team has a tech lead with time for review. If they don't, start with one engineer, not three."
**Respects the reader's time.** Bullet points are fine in technical detail. No fluff paragraphs.
**Understated.** Let the specifics impress. No adjectives doing the selling.

---

## Sounds like

**Email:**
> Saw you're hiring two senior backend engineers for the payments team, and the roles have been open since June.
>
> We run Team Extension for fintech teams on similar stacks (Kotlin, PostgreSQL, Kafka). Before anyone starts we write an onboarding doc for your codebase, so your leads spend their time on review, not setup. Median time to first merged PR across our last 24 engagements is 9 working days.
>
> If it's useful, I can walk your tech lead through how the first two weeks work. 30 minutes, engineer to engineer.
>
> Piotr

**Technical one-pager section:**
> **Week 1:** local environment running, onboarding doc read, first small ticket picked from your backlog.
> **Week 2:** first PR merged. Joins your on-call rotation shadowing if you run one.
> **Week 4:** owning tickets end to end, reviewing teammates' PRs.

---

## Never sounds like

> ~~"Our rockstar developers will supercharge your velocity."~~
> ~~"We leverage cutting-edge technologies."~~
> ~~"Our talented team is passionate about code."~~

---

## Rules

- Technologies capitalised correctly.
- Never claim expertise in a technology not listed in the brief or the industry file.
- Numbers only from MESSAGING.md approved proof points.
- Under 150 words for a cold email. Bullets allowed.
