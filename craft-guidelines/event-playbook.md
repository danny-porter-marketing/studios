# Craft Guide: Event Playbook

**Layer 6 · Load when the asset is an event plan, pre-event outreach, or post-event follow-up**

---

## What this asset is

A complete plan for getting value from one event: who to meet, how to reach them before, what to say on the day, and how to follow up. Events are expensive. A playbook makes sure they produce meetings, not just badge scans.

---

## The shape

```
SECTION 1   Event summary       Name, date, location, audience, why we're going
SECTION 2   Goal                Number of meetings booked, target accounts to meet
SECTION 3   Target list         10 to 30 named accounts, tiered (see abm/account-tiers.md)
SECTION 4   Pre-event outreach  2-touch sequence, sent 3 weeks and 1 week before
SECTION 5   On the day          Who attends, talking points, the one question to ask
SECTION 6   Follow-up           Within 48 hours, personalised to the conversation
SECTION 7   Measurement         Meetings booked, opportunities created within 60 days
```

---

## Rules

- **Pre-event outreach** offers something specific: a 20-minute coffee with Piotr, a short session, a seat at a dinner. Never "stop by our booth".
- **Talking points** are questions, not pitches. The one question for this audience: "What's slipping on your roadmap this quarter?"
- **Follow-up** references the actual conversation. The template has a required field for "what they told us".
- **Measurement** is opportunities created, not leads scanned. Log in the CRM using `revops/crm-conventions.md`.

## Checklist

- [ ] Clear meeting goal set before the event
- [ ] Target list is named accounts, not job titles
- [ ] Follow-up within 48 hours, specific to each conversation
- [ ] Results logged against the campaign
