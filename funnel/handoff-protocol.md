# Marketing to Sales Handoff

How an MQL moves to sales without losing context.

---

## What marketing hands over

Every MQL arrives in the CRM with:

```
Field                 Example
--------------------------------------------------------------
Contact, title        Rui Matos, VP Engineering
Account, tier, score  Halvard Pay, Tier 1, 85
Why now               6 senior backend roles open, UK launch Q2 2027
Entry play            Deadline play
What they engaged     Replied to touch 3 asking about onboarding time
Assets sent           Team Extension one-pager, Ledgerline story
Suggested next step   Technical call with Piotr, invite eng manager
Account file          accounts/halvard-pay.md
```

## Service levels

- **Scoping call request:** sales responds the same working day.
- **Other MQLs:** accepted or rejected within 2 working days.
- **Accepted:** first sales touch within 1 working day of acceptance.

## The first sales touch

References what the contact actually did. Never a generic "thanks for your interest". If they asked a question, the first message answers it.

## Feedback loop

- Sales logs the outcome of every first call: SQL, not now (with a follow-up date), or disqualified (with a reason).
- Marketing reviews outcomes weekly and adds anything new to `use-cases.md`.
- Objections heard on calls go to #corvane-studios so the Friday update picks them up.
