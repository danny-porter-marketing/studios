Onboarding docs don't get read because they're written for the wrong person.

Most are written by someone who already knows the system. So they explain the architecture and skip the part where a new engineer is stuck for a day because a seed script needs an env var nobody mentioned.

The version that gets read has four sections:

1. Get it running. Every command, in order, tested by the last person who joined.
2. Where things live. Ten lines, not a diagram.
3. How we work. Branch names, PR size, who reviews what, when to ask.
4. Who to ask. One named person per area, with permission to interrupt them.

Rule: the newest engineer on the team owns the doc and fixes it on their first day.

It takes about two hours to write the first version. It saves every new joiner about a week.
