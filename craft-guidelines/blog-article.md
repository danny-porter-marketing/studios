# Craft Guide: Blog Article

**Layer 6 · Load when the asset is a blog article, guide, or long-form post on the Corvane site**

---

## What this asset is

A useful article for engineering leaders that answers a real question they search for or ask on calls. Its job is trust and search traffic, not a sale. If the article only makes sense as an ad for Corvane, it has failed.

---

## The shape

```
TITLE         The question or the promise, under 70 characters
INTRO         2 to 3 short paragraphs: the problem and what the reader will get
BODY          3 to 6 H2 sections, each answering one part of the question
TAKEAWAY      A short summary or checklist
CTA           One line, soft, relevant to the article
```

**Length:** 1,000 to 1,800 words.

---

## Rules

- **Pick a real question.** From use-cases.md, sales calls, or search demand. "How long should contractor onboarding take?" beats "Why choose Corvane".
- **First 100 words answer the question directly.** Then expand.
- **Use specifics:** examples, checklists, sample docs, real stacks.
- **Corvane appears at most twice:** once as an example if useful, once in the CTA.
- **SEO:** the main search phrase goes in the title, the first paragraph and one H2. Never stuff it.
- **No invented data.** If you need a statistic and don't have an approved one, make the argument without it.

## Good article ideas

- The contractor onboarding checklist (and why most teams skip it)
- How to evaluate an LLM feature before you ship it
- Team extension vs hiring: an honest comparison
- What to ask a software house before you sign
- How our engineers use AI coding assistants, and where we don't let them

## Checklist

- [ ] Answers a real question in the first 100 words
- [ ] Useful even if the reader never contacts Corvane
- [ ] Two Corvane mentions at most
- [ ] Title under 70 characters, search phrase placed naturally
