Notes

Recommended next steps

  • Pause PS_Remote_Tips_Promo (€30k, zero SQLs) and move the budget to the referral and partner programme and webinars.
  • Add job-title exclusions (recruiter, student, software engineer) to every paid campaign.
  • Make the vendor checklist and the Northrow case study the default paid offers, with 'Contact us' as the main CTA.

What this data can't tell us

  • Attribution is first touch only (Original Source, First Conversion). It credits whoever brought the lead in, not what moved the deal.
  • Won counts are small (3 to 16 per channel), so won revenue by channel is directional. Pipeline and SQLs are the safer read.
  • Leads from July and August have had less time to progress. August SQL rate is 8.4% vs 18.6% in July.
  • SDR and Content spend are flat monthly figures and probably exclude salaries, so their cost per SQL is understated.
  • Partner has leads but no spend line. Paid Social spend isn't split by Meta and LinkedIn.
  • The GBP rate of 1.17 is an assumption. It touches 6 deals.

What was cleaned and why

  • Removed 11 exact duplicate rows.
  • Removed 1 'TOTAL' row with a broken '=SUM()' in Deal Amount.
  • Removed 5 internal test rows (IDs Q0 to Q4, company 'Corvane (internal)', Won at €120,000 each). Left in, they would have added €600,000 of fake won revenue.
  • Parsed 255 dates written MM/DD/YYYY to ISO.
  • Excluded 56 leads created 1 to 9 September, because spend covers Jan to Aug only.
  • Mapped 19 source spellings (e.g. Google, PPC, Paid Search) to 7 channels. Kept Meta vs LinkedIn as a separate platform column.
  • Standardised job-title casing (8 all-caps variants). Set 79 blank countries to 'Unknown'.
  • Converted 6 deal amounts stored as GBP text (e.g. '£53,800') to EUR at an assumed 1.17. No rate was supplied.
  • Spend: two rows for Aug PPC_Dedicated_Team (€3,005 and €3,150). Kept the first; the gap is €3,150 either way.
  • Added 1/0 flags: is_mql, is_sql (Sales Qualified or later, including Lost), is_opp (has a deal), is_won.
