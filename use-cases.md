## Objections and what worked

```
Format:
Objection:
Context:
What worked:
```

**Objection:** "Last vendor sent us juniors with senior titles."
Context: CTO, second call, fintech.
What worked: Offering to let their tech lead run the technical interview with our engineers, and to say no without reason. Trust moved immediately.

**Objection:** "We can't have people outside the EU touch customer data."
Context: Healthtech, procurement stage.
What worked: Confirming all engineers are EU-based and work on client hardware under the client's security policy. Sent the ISO 27001 certificate the same day.

**Objection:** "Your day rate is higher than the other quote."
Context: VP Engineering, final stage.
What worked: Reframing from day rate to time to first merged PR and rework rate. Shared the median of 9 working days. Didn't discount.

## What's working in outbound

```
Format:
[Date] · [Channel] · [Persona]
What worked:
Why we think it worked:
```

**2026-09 · Email · CTO**
What worked: Opening with their own open engineering roles ("You've had a senior platform role open since June").
Why we think it worked: Specific, verifiable, and about their problem. Reply rate roughly double our generic opener.

**2026-09 · LinkedIn · VP Engineering**
What worked: Piotr's post on "the first two weeks decide whether a contractor works out". Several VPs commented with their own horror stories, which became warm conversations.
Why we think it worked: It named a pain everyone has and didn't mention Corvane.

**2026-08 · Email · Head of Product**
What didn't work: Leading with the AI Integration service. Heads of Product replied asking about the roadmap problem, not the AI.
Lesson: For this persona lead with roadmap delivery. Mention AI second.
