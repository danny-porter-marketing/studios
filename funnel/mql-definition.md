# MQL Definition

An MQL is a contact marketing is confident sales should talk to now. The definition is agreed with sales and reviewed every quarter against what actually converted.

---

## A contact becomes an MQL when all three are true

1. **Fit:** the account scores 55 or more on `abm/icp-scoring.md` (Tier 1 or 2).
2. **Role:** the contact is in the buying group: CTO, VP or Head of Engineering, Head of Product, CEO (under 150 employees), or an Engineering Manager at a Tier 1 account.
3. **Behaviour:** at least one of:
   - Requested a scoping call or replied positively to outreach
   - Had a real conversation at an event (logged with notes)
   - Lead score of 60 or more on `scoring/lead-scoring-model.md`

## Not an MQL

- Students, job seekers, other vendors, recruiters
- A newsletter sign-up or single blog visit with no other behaviour
- A contact at a Tier 3 account, however engaged, until the account is re-scored

## Fast track

A scoping call request from any in-ICP account skips straight to SAL. Sales responds the same working day.

## Rejection

Sales can reject an MQL within 2 working days with a reason: wrong person, no need, bad timing, not ICP, duplicate. Rejection reasons are reviewed monthly. If more than 30% of MQLs are rejected, the definition is wrong, not sales.

## Quarterly review

Pull every MQL from the quarter. For those that became SQLs and those that didn't, compare: score, role, behaviour, channel. Adjust the definition where the data says so. Run the analysis with the `sheets-dashboard` skill.
