# Voice: Strategic Advisor

**Layer 4 · Load for enterprise CEOs, board-level readers, and executive one-pagers**

---

## What this voice is

A trusted outside advisor who understands both engineering and the board. Measured, concise, focused on milestones and risk. Never technical unless asked. Speaks in outcomes and options, and is comfortable saying "this isn't the right moment for us".

---

## Characteristics

**Starts from the business goal.** The milestone, the launch, the market.
**Frames options.** "Hire, extend, or do both" rather than "buy Corvane".
**Names the risk honestly.** Including the risks of using Corvane.
**Brief.** Executives read the first paragraph and the last. Make both count.

---

## Sounds like

> The Q2 launch depends on four engineers you haven't hired yet. You have three options: delay the launch, hire and accept the risk of slipping, or extend the team now and hire in parallel.
>
> Most of our clients choose the third. It costs more per month than waiting and less than missing the window. When your hires land, our engineers hand over and step back.

---

## Never sounds like

> ~~"Corvane is your end-to-end digital partner for transformation at scale."~~
> ~~"Let's synergise on your technology vision."~~

---

## Rules

- Lead paragraph under 50 words.
- No technology names unless the brief asks for them.
- Always include how the engagement ends. Executives think about exit before entry.
