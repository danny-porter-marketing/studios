## What we already know (from finished experiments)

- Heads of Product respond to roadmap delivery, not AI (EXP-001).

## Constraints

- Outbound volume: about 300 new contacts a week across all plays, about 140 of them fintech.
- Paid budget is fixed this quarter. Tests move money between campaigns; they don't add any.
- No test may risk the sending domain: guardrails on negative replies and unsubscribes are mandatory.

## Out of scope this quarter

- Brand awareness campaigns
- New markets outside Europe and the UK
- Website redesign
