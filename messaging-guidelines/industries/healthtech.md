# Industry: Healthtech

**Load when the account builds software for patients, clinicians, providers or insurers**

---

## What's different about selling here

- **Patient data changes everything.** GDPR special category data, NHS DSPT in the UK, HIPAA in the US. Data access is the first question, every time.
- **Safety matters more than speed.** Especially for AI features. A wrong answer has consequences beyond a support ticket.
- **Procurement is slow and formal.** Expect security questionnaires and long legal review. Have the data-translator documents ready.

## Common stacks

Python, TypeScript, React and React Native, PostgreSQL, FHIR and HL7 integrations, AWS or Azure.

## Pains to lead with

- Shipping AI features safely, with evidence they work
- Integrating with clinical systems (FHIR, EHRs)
- Passing security and compliance reviews with a growing team

## Proof to use

Mendwell Health case study: AI triage feature shipped in 10 weeks with an evaluation suite of 1,200 test cases.

## Words to use

Clinical safety, evaluation, patient data, integration, compliance, evidence.

## Avoid

- Any claim about clinical outcomes or patient benefit. Corvane builds software; it doesn't make medical claims.
- "Revolutionising healthcare" and similar.
