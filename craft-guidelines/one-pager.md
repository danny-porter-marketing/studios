# Craft Guide: One-Pager

**Layer 6 · Load when the asset is a one-pager, leave-behind, or service sheet**

---

## What this asset is

A single page someone reads after a call, forwards to their boss, or prints for a meeting. It must make sense to a person who has never heard of Corvane, in under two minutes.

---

## The shape

```
BLOCK 1   Headline + one-line subhead       The outcome for this reader
BLOCK 2   The problem                       2 to 3 sentences
BLOCK 3   What Corvane does                 3 bullets, bold claim + one line each
BLOCK 4   How it works                      A 4-step timeline
BLOCK 5   Proof                             One short proof story or 3 numbers
BLOCK 6   Practical facts                   Location, overlap hours, security, contract basics
BLOCK 7   Next step                         One action and a named contact
```

**Length:** 250 to 400 words. If it doesn't fit one A4 page, cut.

---

## Persona variants

- **CTO / VP Engineering:** BLOCK 4 gets the most space. Week-by-week detail.
- **CEO:** BLOCK 1 and BLOCK 5 carry it. Milestone language. Mention the exit.
- **Procurement:** use the data-translator voice. BLOCK 6 expands into a table of compliance and contract facts.

## Checklist

- [ ] Fits on one page
- [ ] Makes sense to a stranger
- [ ] Named contact and one clear next step
- [ ] Practical facts are all from MESSAGING.md
