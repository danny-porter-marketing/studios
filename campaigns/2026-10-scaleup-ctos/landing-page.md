## 1. Hero

**Headline:** Your roadmap is ahead of your hiring.

**Subheadline:** Senior engineers who join your team, work in your codebase and ship from week two. For CTOs who can't wait six months for the next hire.

**CTA:** Book a 30-minute scoping call

## 2. Problem

**Headline:** The launch date holds. The hiring plan doesn't.

The round was raised on a roadmap. The senior roles behind it have been open for months.

Contractors look like the answer until they arrive. Your leads lose weeks to setup questions, the first pull requests come back for rework, and by the time anyone is productive the quarter is gone.

## 3. What changes

**Headline:** Capacity that doesn't need babysitting.

- **Senior only.** Every engineer has 6+ years of commercial experience in product companies. You interview each one and can say no.
- **Onboarded before day one.** We write an onboarding playbook for your codebase with your tech lead, so week one is spent on tickets, not setup.
- **Shipped work, reported weekly.** Every Friday you see what merged, what's in review and what's blocked.

## 4. How it works

**Headline:** From scoping call to first merged PR.

- **Day 0 · Scoping call.** 30 minutes with an engineer: your stack, your team, what's slipping.
- **Day 5 · Profiles.** 2 to 3 senior candidates per role. You interview them.
- **Week 1 · Playbook.** Onboarding doc written with your tech lead before they start.
- **Week 2 · First merge.** Median 9 working days to first merged pull request.

## 5. Proof

**Headline:** What it looks like in practice.

- Ledgerline grew from 3 to 11 engineers in 5 months and launched card issuing in 3 markets.
- 82% of clients extend after the first 6 months.
- Average engagement length: 2.5 years.

## 6. Objections

**Headline:** What CTOs ask us first.

**"We tried outsourcing. It went badly."**
Most engagements go wrong in the first two weeks, during onboarding. That's why we write the playbook before day one and track time to first merged PR. Ask us for the numbers from our last 24 engagements.

**"We'd rather hire."**
So would we, in your position. Most clients do both: we cover the gap while you hire, then hand over with everything documented. Notice is 30 days per engineer.

## 7. Final CTA

**Headline:** Keep the roadmap moving while you hire.

**Body:** 30 minutes with an engineer, not a salesperson.

**CTA:** Book a 30-minute scoping call
