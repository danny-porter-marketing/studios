# Craft Guide: LinkedIn Post

**Layer 6 · Load when the asset is a LinkedIn post or carousel in Marta's or Piotr's name, or on the Corvane page**

---

## What this asset is

A short post that earns attention without paid distribution. Personal posts (Marta, Piotr) build authority and warm audiences. They are not ads and they don't end with a hard sell.

---

## Post types

```
Type 01   Observation    Something true from the real world. No product needed.
Type 02   Contrarian     A common belief, challenged with a specific argument.
Type 03   Story          A real moment (anonymised) with a lesson.
Type 04   Framework      A practical model or checklist the reader can use.
Type 05   Carousel       Multi-slide argument, one idea per slide.
```

Decide the type before writing.

---

## The shape (types 01 to 04)

```
Line 1        Hook        One sentence. Makes the reader stop.
Lines 2 to n  Body        The argument, story or framework.
Last line     Landing     The implication, or a question you want answered.
```

**Length:** 120 to 250 words.
**Formatting:** short paragraphs, white space, numbered lists allowed for frameworks.

### The hook
- One sentence, two at most
- A claim, a tension or a surprising specific
- Doesn't start with "I"
- No rhetorical question, no filler opener

> Good: "The first two weeks decide whether a contractor works out."
> Good: "We lost a deal last month because we wouldn't discount."
> Bad: ~~"Excited to share some thoughts on team scaling!"~~
> Bad: ~~"What if your vendor is the problem?"~~

### The landing
A clear implication, or a question you'd genuinely want answers to. Never "Agree?" or "Thoughts?".

---

## Carousel (type 05)

```
Slide 1        Hook, under 10 words
Slides 2 to 7  One idea per slide, under 25 words each
Final slide    The takeaway, plus who it's from
```

Post copy for a carousel: two or three lines that set it up.

---

## Rules

- Corvane mentioned in at most one post in four
- No hashtag strings (two at most), no emoji, no em dashes
- Fictional or anonymised client details only, unless it's an approved case study
- Match the named voice file exactly

## Checklist

- [ ] Type chosen
- [ ] Hook works on its own before "see more"
- [ ] One idea, fully made
- [ ] Ends with a landing, not a pitch
