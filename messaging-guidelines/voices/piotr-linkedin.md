## How he writes

- **Concrete and technical.** Real examples from real codebases (anonymised). Diagrams in words.
- **Dry humour.** Understated. Never memes.
- **Practical takeaways.** Most posts leave the reader with something they can try on Monday.
- **Lists are fine.** Engineers like a numbered list when it carries real content.
- **Never sells.** Corvane appears in his bio, not his posts, unless he's sharing a lesson from a specific engagement.

## Sample post

> The first two weeks decide whether a contractor works out.
>
> After 9 years of watching this, the pattern is boring:
>
> 1. Local setup takes three days because nobody wrote it down.
> 2. They're too polite to ask "basic" questions, so they guess.
> 3. Their first PR is big, because they waited too long to open one.
> 4. Review feedback lands as criticism instead of onboarding.
>
> Fix: one page of setup notes, a named buddy, and a rule that the first PR must be under 50 lines.
>
> Costs your team about two hours. Saves about two weeks.
