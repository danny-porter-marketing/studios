# Service: Product Delivery

**Load when the brief is about a squad owning an outcome**

---

## What it is

A cross-functional Corvane squad (typically 3 to 5 engineers, a QA engineer and a delivery lead, with design if needed) takes ownership of a defined product outcome. The client sets the goal and the constraints. The squad plans, builds, tests and ships, then hands over with documentation.

## Who it's for

Companies with a clear outcome and no internal team free to own it: a new product line, a migration, a market launch, a platform rebuild.

## How it works

1. **Scoping workshop (half a day):** outcome, constraints, success measure, risks.
2. **Two-week discovery:** technical plan, estimate range, first slice defined.
3. **Delivery in two-week cycles:** demo at the end of each, shipped-work report every Friday.
4. **Handover:** documentation, recorded walkthroughs, pairing with the client team that will own it.

## Key messages

- One outcome, one squad, one accountable delivery lead.
- You see working software every two weeks.
- Built to be handed over, not to keep you dependent.

## Not a fit when

- The outcome isn't defined yet. Start with a paid discovery instead.
- The client wants to direct every engineer daily. That's Team Extension.
