## Snapshot
- What they do: B2B payments for merchants and marketplaces. Pay Later (invoice on 30 to 90 day terms, instalments, a digital trade account), Pay Now (account-to-account instant payments, launched July 2025), and MonduCard (business credit card with 45-day terms, Germany only). Integrates into merchants' ERP and CRM systems and the banking layer, including virtual IBANs. [S1, S2, S3, S4]
- Industry: Fintech (B2B buy now, pay later and payments).
- Employees: about 100 to 145, third-party estimates only. Revelio: ~143 (Mar 2026), down from 191 in 2023 (about 25% fewer). Tracxn: 99 (Aug 2026). Rocketlist: 140. [S5, S6, S7]
- Engineering size: Revelio puts Engineering at 35.7% of staff, roughly 51 people (estimate on an estimate). [S5]
- Funding: Series A, $43m (May 2022, led by Valar Ventures, with Cherry Ventures and FinTech Collective), extended by $13m (January 2023) to $56m. Debt: EUR 100m facility from J.P. Morgan Payments (10 December 2025) for scaling B2B payments and European expansion. No equity round since 2023. [S8, S9, S10, S11, S6]
- HQ and engineering locations: Berlin HQ. Other locations listed: Amsterdam, Shanghai. 88.5% of staff in Western Europe. Engineering location not confirmed beyond Berlin. [S5, S12, S13]
- Markets: Germany, Austria, Netherlands, UK, then France and Belgium. An aggregator claims six recent market entries (unconfirmed). [S14, S15]
- Stack: not confirmed. The only engineering posting found is from January 2022 (Senior Software Engineer, Backend, Berlin) asking for Go, Ruby or Python and microservices. Nothing current. [S16]
