# Industry: Fintech

**Load when the account or brief is in payments, lending, banking, card issuing, wealth, insurance tech or crypto infrastructure**

---

## What's different about selling here

- **Deadlines are external.** Regulators, card schemes and banking partners set dates the company can't move. That makes urgency real, and it's the strongest lever we have. Lead with it.
- **Security and compliance come first.** Expect questions about data access, EU residency, background checks and audit trails before anyone asks about engineering.
- **Senior backend talent is the bottleneck.** Payments, ledgers and reconciliation need engineers who've done it before.

## Common stacks

Kotlin or Java on the JVM, Go, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Kubernetes on AWS or GCP. React and TypeScript on the front end. Only mention stacks the account actually uses.

## Pains to lead with

- A regulatory or partner deadline the current team can't hit
- Launching in new markets (new currencies, new rails, new regulators)
- Ledger and reconciliation work nobody on the team has done before
- Senior backend roles open for months

## Proof to use

Ledgerline case study: 3 engineers to 11 in 5 months, card issuing launched in 3 markets.

## Words to use

Regulatory deadline, market launch, ledger, reconciliation, compliance, audit trail, card scheme, licence.

## Avoid

- Implying Corvane handles compliance itself. The client owns compliance; Corvane engineers work within it.
- "Disrupting finance" and similar.
