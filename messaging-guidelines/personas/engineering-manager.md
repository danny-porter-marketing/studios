# Persona: Engineering Manager

**Layer 3 · Load when the reader is an Engineering Manager, Team Lead or Tech Lead**
**Paired voice:** `engineer-to-engineer.md`

---

## Who they are

Leads one team of 4 to 10 engineers. Still close to the code. Will be the person Corvane engineers actually work with every day. Rarely the signer, often the person whose opinion decides whether the engagement is extended at month six.

Matters most after the deal: in onboarding, in the first month, at renewal. Also a strong source of inbound when they've had a bad vendor experience and want better.

---

## What they care about

- Their team's sprint going well
- New people who need little hand-holding
- Code review load staying manageable
- Not becoming a full-time onboarding coordinator

## What they fear

- Spending a month answering basic questions
- Reviewing low-quality pull requests every day
- Contractors who don't care about the codebase because they won't be around to maintain it

---

## How they buy

They don't, directly. They influence. They'll test what Corvane says by asking very practical questions: how do your engineers handle code review feedback, do they write tests, will they join on-call?

---

## Language they use

```
PR                 Review              Tests               On-call
Pairing            Ticket              Stand-up            Context
```

---

## What Corvane means to them

Teammates who arrive with the setup already working, read the onboarding playbook, and ask good questions instead of basic ones. Pull requests that pass review the way a senior colleague's would.

**The frame that lands:** "They'll have read your codebase docs before day one. Your job is code review, not babysitting."

---

## What they ignore

- Commercial terms
- Company strategy
- Marketing language of any kind
