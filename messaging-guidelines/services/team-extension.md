# Service: Team Extension

**Load when the brief is about adding engineers to a client's existing team**

---

## What it is

1 to 6 senior Corvane engineers join the client's team. They work in the client's repo, tools and rituals, report to the client's engineering manager day to day, and are managed by Corvane for everything else (performance, development, replacement).

## Who it's for

Teams whose roadmap is ahead of their hiring. Best when the client has a tech lead with time to review code and set direction.

## How it works

1. **Scoping call (30 min):** with a Corvane engineer. Stack, team, what's slipping.
2. **Profiles in 5 working days:** 2 to 3 candidates per role. The client interviews and can say no.
3. **Onboarding playbook:** Corvane writes it with the client's tech lead before day one.
4. **Week 2:** first merged PR (median 9 working days).
5. **Every Friday:** shipped-work report.
6. **Exit:** 30 days' notice per engineer, handover documented.

## Key messages

- Senior only, and you interview every engineer.
- Your team, your process, your architecture decisions.
- First merged PR in week two.

## Not a fit when

- There's no one on the client side to review code or set direction. Recommend Product Delivery instead.
- The client needs one engineer for under 3 months. Onboarding cost outweighs the benefit.

Saying this openly is part of the brand. Use it.
