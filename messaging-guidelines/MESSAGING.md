## Positioning

**For** CTOs and engineering leaders at tech companies whose roadmap has outgrown their hiring,
**Corvane is** an engineering delivery partner
**that** puts senior engineers inside your team who ship from week two,
**unlike** staffing agencies and offshore vendors, who sell hours and leave onboarding to you.

**Category:** Engineering delivery partner.
**Canonical one-liner:** Senior engineers who ship in your codebase from week two.

---

## The narrative arc

Use this order in any long-form asset (landing page, case study, deck, long post).

1. **The gap.** The roadmap is ahead of hiring. A senior hire takes 4 to 6 months to find and another 3 to get productive. The board doesn't wait.
2. **The usual fix fails.** Agencies send whoever is free. Engineers arrive with no context, the internal team spends weeks onboarding them, and the work comes back needing rework. Most vendor engagements fail at onboarding.
3. **What changes it.** Senior engineers only, a written onboarding playbook for your codebase before day one, and delivery reported as shipped work, not hours.
4. **What it looks like.** First pull request in week two. Your team stays in charge of architecture. You see what shipped every Friday.
5. **The ask.** A 30-minute scoping call with an engineer, not a salesperson.

---

## Core messages

### Message 1: Senior only
Every Corvane engineer has at least 6 years of commercial experience and has shipped production code inside a product company. No juniors learning on your budget.

### Message 2: Onboarded in days
Before an engineer starts, Corvane writes an onboarding playbook for your codebase: architecture notes, local setup, conventions, who to ask. Median time to first merged pull request is 9 working days.

### Message 3: Delivery you can see
Every Friday the client gets a shipped-work report: what merged, what's in review, what's blocked and why. No timesheets.

### Message 4: You stay in charge
Corvane engineers work in your repo, your tools, your rituals. Your tech leads own architecture decisions. We add capacity, not a second opinion you didn't ask for.

## What Corvane never says

- That it's the cheapest option
- That it has "a pool" or "a bench" of developers
- That AI will replace engineers, or that Corvane uses AI to cut headcount
- Anything about a competitor by name
- Any claim of guaranteed outcomes, deadlines or savings
