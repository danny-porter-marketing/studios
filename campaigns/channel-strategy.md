# Channel Strategy

Where Corvane spends time and money, and why. Reviewed quarterly against the reporting framework.

---

## Principles

1. **Warm beats cold.** Referrals and partner intros convert far better than anything else. Invest in them first.
2. **Proof beats reach.** Case studies and technical content outperform brand awareness for this buyer.
3. **Few accounts, done properly,** beat many accounts done badly. ABM over volume.
4. **Measure by SQLs and pipeline,** never by leads.

## What we don't do

- Broad paid search on generic terms. Expensive and full of people looking for freelancers.
- Booth-only event presence.
- Buying contact lists.
- Gated content for anything short. Only gate genuinely substantial guides.

## Budget split (starting point, adjust from data)

Rebalance quarterly based on cost per SQL and pipeline by channel from the reporting framework. Any channel with spend and no SQLs for two quarters is cut or rebuilt.
