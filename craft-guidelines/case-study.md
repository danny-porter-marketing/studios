# Craft Guide: Case Study

**Layer 6 · Load when the asset is a client case study, a short proof story, or a case study section in another asset**

---

## What this asset is

The story of one client engagement, told so a reader in a similar situation sees themselves in it. Case studies are the strongest proof Corvane has. They are also the easiest place to break trust, so every fact must come from MESSAGING.md approved proof points or the operator's source material.

---

## The shape

```
SECTION 1   Headline        The outcome in under 12 words
SECTION 2   At a glance     Client, industry, service, duration, 2 to 3 headline numbers
SECTION 3   The situation   What was at stake and why the client couldn't do it alone
SECTION 4   What we did     The approach, in 3 to 5 concrete steps
SECTION 5   What changed    Outcomes, with numbers
SECTION 6   The detail      One technical or human detail that makes it real
SECTION 7   Quote           Only if supplied by the operator. Never invented.
SECTION 8   CTA             One line: who should talk to us about this
```

---

## Rules

- **Headline states the outcome:** "Card issuing live in 3 markets in 5 months."
- **The situation names the stakes:** the deadline, the risk, what failure would have cost.
- **What we did shows the client in charge:** "Ledgerline's platform lead set the architecture; our engineers built the issuing services."
- **Numbers:** approved proof points only. If the operator supplies new numbers, flag them so they can be added to MESSAGING.md.
- **Quotes:** never write one. If there isn't a supplied quote, leave the section out.
- **Length:** 500 to 800 words for a full case study. 60 to 100 words for a short proof story.

## Short proof story shape (for emails, ads, landing pages)

```
[Client] needed [outcome] by [deadline/constraint]. [What Corvane did in one sentence]. [Result with approved number].
```

> Ledgerline needed to launch card issuing in three markets with a team of three backend engineers. We grew the team to 11 in five months, working inside their platform team. Card issuing went live in all three markets.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every number traced to MESSAGING.md or the operator's source
- [ ] No invented quote
- [ ] Client shown in charge, Corvane shown as capacity and craft
- [ ] Reader can tell in 10 seconds whether it's relevant to them
