# Multi-Thread Strategy

Corvane deals involve 3 to 6 people. Deals that rely on one contact stall when that person is busy, sceptical or leaves. For Tier 1 accounts, reach several people with messages that fit each of them and point the same way.

---

## The buying group

```
Role                   What they decide              What they need from us
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CTO                    Yes or no                     Trust, evidence, low risk
VP Engineering         Recommendation, fit           Process detail, a pilot
Head of Product        Urgency, priority             Roadmap outcomes
Engineering Manager    Day-to-day success, renewal   Low hand-holding, good PRs
CEO (under 150)        Final approval                Milestones, clean exit
Procurement            Contract, risk                Complete documents, fast
```

## Sequence of threads

1. **Entry thread:** the person the entry play targets (usually CTO or VP Engineering).
2. **Pain thread (week 1 to 2):** Head of Product, with the roadmap angle. Gives the entry contact internal pressure to act.
3. **Ground thread (after first meeting):** Engineering Manager, invited to the technical session.
4. **Approval thread (proposal stage):** CEO or procurement, with the matching one-pager.

## Rules

- **Same story, different angle.** Every thread references the same problem. Never tell two people different things.
- **No going around the champion.** Once someone is engaged, tell them who else we'd like to involve and why. Ask for the intro.
- **Map it.** Each Tier 1 account file lists contacts, their role in the deal, last touch and status.
- **One sender per person.** Piotr for technical roles, Marta for CEO, sales lead for procurement.
