# Account Entry Plays

A play is a pre-built way into an account, triggered by a specific signal. Match the strongest signal in the account file to a play, then run the `outbound-sequence` skill with that play.

---

## Play 1: The open roles play
**Trigger:** 2+ senior engineering roles open, or any open 90+ days.
**First contact:** VP Engineering or CTO.
**Sender:** Piotr.
**Angle:** "Those roles will take months to fill. Here's how teams keep the roadmap moving meanwhile."
**Offer:** 30 minutes with an engineer on how the first two weeks work.
**Asset:** Team Extension one-pager with the week-by-week timeline.

## Play 2: The fresh funding play
**Trigger:** Series A, B or C announced in the last 6 months.
**First contact:** CEO (under 150 people) or CTO.
**Sender:** Marta.
**Angle:** "The round was raised on a roadmap. The hiring plan usually lags it by two quarters."
**Offer:** A short call on hitting the next milestone without waiting to hire.
**Asset:** Industry case study (Ledgerline for fintech, Northrow for SaaS).

## Play 3: The new leader play
**Trigger:** New CTO or VP Engineering in the last 6 months.
**First contact:** The new leader.
**Sender:** Piotr.
**Angle:** New leaders inherit a roadmap and a team they didn't build, and get about 90 days to show progress.
**Offer:** Their first quick win: capacity on the item that's been slipping longest.
**Timing:** Wait until week 4 to 6 in the role. Earlier is noise.

## Play 4: The deadline play
**Trigger:** A dated public commitment: regulatory deadline, market launch, partner integration, migration end date.
**First contact:** CTO, with the Head of Product as second thread.
**Sender:** Piotr.
**Angle:** The date can't move. Capacity can.
**Asset:** Short proof story with a similar deadline.

## Play 5: The AI pressure play
**Trigger:** Public AI announcement or roadmap item, an AI-related job ad with no existing ML team, or a board or investor push mentioned publicly.
**First contact:** CTO (lead with evaluation and risk), Head of Product second (lead with the roadmap, not AI; see use-cases.md).
**Sender:** Piotr.
**Angle:** "Define what working means before you build."
**Asset:** Mendwell Health proof story, AI Integration one-pager.

## Play 6: The warm path play
**Trigger:** Mutual connection, past client contact who moved companies, partner intro.
**First contact:** Whoever the warm path leads to.
**Sender:** Whoever has the relationship.
**Angle:** Ask for the intro or reconnect personally. No sequence until the warm path has been tried.

---

## Choosing between plays

If several signals apply, use this priority: Warm path, Deadline, New leader, Open roles, Funding, AI pressure. A warm path always wins. A dated deadline beats a general need.
