# Persona: Head of Product

**Layer 3 · Load when the reader is a Head of Product, VP Product, CPO, or Group Product Manager**
**Paired voice:** `founder-direct.md`

---

## Who they are

Owns the roadmap: what gets built, in what order, and why. Accountable for product outcomes such as adoption, retention and revenue from new features. Depends on engineering capacity they don't control. Usually the person most frustrated by the gap between what's planned and what ships.

Influencer and champion more often than signer. Strong ally for Product Delivery, where a squad owns an outcome on their roadmap.

---

## What they care about

- Getting the roadmap shipped in the quarter it was promised
- Outcomes, not output: features that move a metric
- Saying yes to the sales team and the CEO more often
- Not having engineering capacity become the excuse for everything

## What they fear

- Another quarter where half the roadmap slips
- A vendor that builds exactly what was specced, including the bad ideas
- Owning a feature nobody on the internal team understands after the vendor leaves

---

## How they buy

They bring the problem to the CTO or VP Engineering and push for a decision. They respond to stories about outcomes shipped, clear scoping, and evidence that the vendor thinks about the user, not just the ticket.

**Lesson from use-cases.md:** lead with roadmap delivery. AI is a second message for this persona, never the opener.

---

## Language they use

```
Roadmap            Discovery           Outcome             Adoption
Prioritisation     Quarter             Launch              Retention
Backlog            Trade-off           Customer problem    Experiment
```

---

## What Corvane means to them

The roadmap items that keep slipping actually ship. A squad that asks "why" before building, and leaves the work documented so the internal team can own it.

**The frame that lands:** "The roadmap items that keep sliding to next quarter, shipped this quarter, by a squad that leaves them documented for your team."

---

## What they ignore

- Engineering process detail
- Technology lists
- Day rates and contract terms
