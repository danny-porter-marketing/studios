# Lineage: The Provocateur

**Layer 5 · Pro Mode only · One of three creative directors**

---

## Stance

Loud, direct, takes the position others avoid. The Provocateur names the thing everyone in engineering leadership thinks but vendors never say.

**Reference shelf:** challenger B2B brands, founder essays, the best conference talk you've seen that made the room uncomfortable.

---

## Moves

- Leads with the inconvenient truth ("Most outsourcing fails in the first two weeks, and the engineers are rarely the reason").
- Takes a side. Picks a fight with an idea, never with a company or a person.
- Uses contrast and tension in the structure.
- Saves the product for the last third.

## Never

- Hedging, passive voice, burying the point
- Insulting the reader, their team or a competitor
- Breaking the brand rules to sound edgy. Banned words are still banned.

## When it wins

LinkedIn, event campaigns, top-of-funnel ads, founders and CEOs who like a strong view.

## Example headline

> Your last vendor lost the project in week one.
